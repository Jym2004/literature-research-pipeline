# Optional Obsidian Topic Synthesis

Use Obsidian only after Zotero paper import and reading-card write-back. Keep Zotero as the canonical store for individual papers, attachments, and reading cards.

## Enable When Requested

Create an Obsidian note when the user asks for:

- a topic-level literature map
- a reading sequence or study plan
- a method comparison
- a reproduction roadmap
- a research-direction judgment
- a durable summary in their vault

## Avoid Duplication

Do not copy every Zotero reading card into Obsidian. Create cross-paper synthesis and link back to Zotero items, DOI URLs, arXiv URLs, or publisher pages.

## Suggested Outputs

```text
<topic> literature map.md
<topic> reading plan.md
<topic> method comparison.md
<topic> reproduction roadmap.md
<topic> current research judgment.md
```

## Suggested Structure

```markdown
# <Topic>

## Scope
[Search scope, dates, and sources]

## Core Papers
[Grouped links to Zotero items or stable source URLs]

## Method Families
[Cross-paper comparison]

## Open Questions
[Gaps and unresolved issues]

## Reading Or Reproduction Order
[Prioritized next steps]
```

Use Obsidian MCP for search, create, and update operations when it is available. Reuse an existing topic note when one is clearly the right home.
